Pogo is an online gaming platform that offers a wide selection of casual games, puzzles, card games, word games, and other entertainment options. Players who have a paid Club Pogo subscription may eventually decide to cancel their membership because they no longer use the service, want to reduce recurring expenses, or simply want to stop automatic renewal.

Canceling a Pogo subscription is generally straightforward when you use the official cancellation process. According to the current EA Help instructions, Club Pogo subscribers can begin the cancellation process by visiting ``www.pogo.com/cancel``, signing in, and following the cancellation prompts. After cancellation, players can continue using their subscription benefits until the current subscription period ends. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

This guide explains how to cancel a Pogo Games subscription step by step, what happens after cancellation, how to confirm that your membership has been canceled, and what to do if you cannot find the cancellation option.

Understanding Your Pogo Subscription
------------------------------------

Before canceling, it is helpful to understand what type of Pogo account you have.

Pogo distinguishes between free players and Club Pogo members. Current EA community guidance explains that a free player does not have an active paid subscription, while an account identified as Club Pogo has a paid membership. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

If you do not have an active paid subscription, there is nothing to cancel. You can continue using your free Pogo account.

If you have an active Club Pogo membership, you can use the official cancellation process to stop the subscription from renewing.

Why You May Want to Cancel Your Pogo Subscription
---------------------------------------------------

There are several common reasons why a player may decide to cancel a Pogo membership.

You may no longer play Pogo games regularly, may want to switch to free games, or may not want another subscription charge on your payment method. You may also be testing another gaming service or simply want to take a break from Pogo.

Whatever your reason, you do not need to delete your entire EA or Pogo account just to stop a paid Club Pogo subscription.

Canceling the subscription and deleting an account are separate actions. If you want to keep your Pogo account, games, progress, or other account information, use the subscription cancellation process rather than requesting account deletion.

Step 1: Go to the Official Pogo Cancellation Page
-------------------------------------------------

The easiest way to start the current cancellation process is to visit the official Pogo cancellation page::

    https://www.pogo.com/cancel

The current EA Help article specifically directs Club Pogo subscribers to this address to begin cancellation. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Using the official Pogo or EA website is important. Avoid entering your account credentials into websites that claim to provide Pogo cancellation services.

A legitimate cancellation process should not require you to give your password to an unrelated third party.

Step 2: Sign In to Your Pogo Account
------------------------------------

After opening the cancellation page, sign in using the account associated with your Club Pogo subscription.

Make sure that you are using the correct account. This is particularly important if you have more than one EA or Pogo account.

If you sign in to an account that does not have a paid Club Pogo membership, you may not see a cancellation option.

For security reasons, EA may ask you to enter your password again during the cancellation process. The current official EA Help instructions specifically mention that re-entering the password may be required. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Step 3: Select "Continue Cancellation"
-------------------------------------

After signing in, look for the **Continue Cancellation** option.

Select it to move forward with the cancellation process.

The wording and appearance of the Pogo website can change over time, but the current official EA instructions identify **Continue Cancellation** as the next step after signing in. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

If you do not see this option, check that you are signed into the correct account and that your account actually has an active Club Pogo subscription.

Step 4: Complete or Skip the Cancellation Survey
------------------------------------------------

Pogo may ask you to complete an optional survey about why you are canceling your membership.

The survey may ask you to select a reason for cancellation. You can provide feedback if you want to explain why you are leaving.

According to EA's current instructions, the survey is optional. If you want to complete it, select your cancellation reason and choose the option to submit the survey and cancel the subscription. If you do not want to complete the survey, you can choose the cancellation option that skips it. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The survey is not required simply to cancel your subscription.

Step 5: Confirm the Cancellation
--------------------------------

Carefully review the final cancellation screen before confirming.

Make sure that you are canceling the correct Club Pogo subscription. If everything looks correct, complete the cancellation process.

Do not close the browser immediately after selecting the cancellation option. Wait for the website to display a confirmation that the subscription has been canceled.

The official EA Help instructions state that you will receive an email confirming that your subscription was canceled. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Step 6: Check Your Confirmation Email
------------------------------------

After canceling, check the email address associated with your Pogo or EA account.

Look for a cancellation confirmation from Pogo or EA.

The confirmation email is useful because it provides evidence that you completed the cancellation process.

If you do not immediately see the message, check your spam, junk, promotions, or other filtered email folders.

You should keep the confirmation email until you are certain that the subscription has ended and no further renewal charge is expected.

Step 7: Understand What Happens After Cancellation
---------------------------------------------------

One of the most important things to understand is that canceling your Pogo subscription does not necessarily mean that your access ends immediately.

According to the current EA Help information, after canceling a Club Pogo subscription, you can continue playing until the end of your existing subscription period. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

For example, suppose your subscription is already paid through the end of the month and you cancel halfway through that period. You may continue using the subscription benefits until the paid period expires.

The cancellation primarily prevents the subscription from continuing into another billing period.

Therefore, do not be surprised if you can still access Club Pogo benefits after completing the cancellation.

Step 8: Check Your Account After Cancellation
---------------------------------------------

After receiving the cancellation confirmation, you can sign in to your Pogo account again and review your membership information.

Check for information indicating that the subscription will not renew.

It is also useful to note the date when your current subscription period ends.

If your membership remains available until that date, this does not necessarily mean the cancellation failed. It may simply mean that you are still within the period you have already paid for.

What If You Cannot Find the Cancel Option?
------------------------------------------

Some users may sign in to Pogo and discover that there is no cancellation link.

One possible reason is that the account does not have an active paid Club Pogo subscription.

Current EA community guidance recommends checking the account type under the Billing and Subscription section. If the account shows **Free Player**, there is no active paid subscription to cancel. If it shows **Club Pogo**, the account has an active paid subscription and should provide a cancellation option. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

If you believe you have a paid subscription but cannot find the cancellation option, verify that you are signed into the account that originally purchased the membership.

You can also use official EA Help support if the problem continues.

Do Not Delete Your Account Just to Cancel
------------------------------------------

Canceling a Club Pogo subscription is different from deleting your EA or Pogo account.

If your goal is simply to stop recurring membership charges, do not delete your entire account unless you specifically want to permanently remove the account.

Deleting an account can have consequences for your access to games, purchases, subscriptions, progress, badges, and other account-related information. An EA community response also distinguishes account deletion from Club Pogo membership cancellation. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

If you want to keep your account but stop paying for Club Pogo, use the subscription cancellation procedure.

What If You Cannot Access the Account?
--------------------------------------

If you cannot sign in to the account that has the subscription, first use the official EA account-recovery options.

Do not create a new account simply to cancel the old subscription. The new account will not necessarily contain the membership you are trying to cancel.

If you cannot recover access to the account, contact official EA support and explain the situation.

EA's current cancellation guidance also provides a support route for situations where a subscription belonging to someone who has passed away cannot be canceled because the account cannot be accessed. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

How to Avoid Future Subscription Charges
----------------------------------------

After completing the cancellation process, take a few simple precautions.

First, save your cancellation confirmation email. Second, make a note of the subscription end date. Third, check your payment statement around the date when the next renewal would normally occur.

If the subscription was successfully canceled, another recurring renewal should not be expected.

If you see a charge that you believe occurred after a successful cancellation, compare the charge date with your cancellation date and confirmation information before contacting support.

What to Do If You Are Charged After Cancellation
------------------------------------------------

If you receive another Pogo subscription charge after canceling, do not immediately assume that the cancellation failed.

First, verify the following:

* You canceled the correct Pogo account.
* The cancellation process was completed.
* You received the confirmation email.
* The charge corresponds to the same subscription.
* The charge date occurred after the cancellation.
* You do not have another Pogo membership under a different account.

If everything appears correct and you still believe the charge is incorrect, contact official EA support.

Keep your cancellation confirmation and payment information available. Do not publish your complete card number, password, security codes, or other sensitive information when asking for help.

Tips for a Smooth Pogo Cancellation
-----------------------------------

Following a few simple practices can make the cancellation process easier.

#. Use the official Pogo cancellation page.
#. Sign in to the correct Pogo or EA account.
#. Confirm that the account has a Club Pogo membership.
#. Select **Continue Cancellation**.
#. Complete the optional survey or skip it.
#. Finish the cancellation process.
#. Wait for the cancellation confirmation.
#. Check your email for confirmation.
#. Save the confirmation message.
#. Note the date when your current subscription period ends.
#. Do not delete your entire account unless that is your actual goal.

Frequently Asked Questions
---------------------------

**Can I cancel my Pogo subscription online?**

Yes. The current official EA Help instructions direct Club Pogo subscribers to ``www.pogo.com/cancel`` to begin the cancellation process. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

**Do I need to provide a reason for canceling?**

Pogo may display an optional cancellation survey. You can provide a reason or skip the survey and continue with cancellation. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

**Can I still play after canceling?**

Yes. According to current EA Help information, you can continue playing until your existing subscription period ends. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

**How do I know that my subscription was canceled?**

EA states that you will receive an email confirming the cancellation. Keep this email for your records. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

**Why can I not find a cancellation button?**

You may be signed into an account that does not have an active paid subscription. Current EA community guidance recommends checking whether your account type is listed as **Free Player** or **Club Pogo**. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

**Does canceling my subscription delete my Pogo account?**

No. Canceling a Club Pogo subscription and deleting an EA or Pogo account are separate actions. If you only want to stop recurring membership charges, cancel the subscription instead of deleting the account.

**What if I cannot access my Pogo account?**

Use official EA account-recovery tools first. If you still cannot access the account or cancel the subscription, contact official EA support for assistance.

Conclusion
----------

Canceling a Pogo Games subscription is a relatively simple process when you use the official Pogo and EA resources. The current procedure begins at ``www.pogo.com/cancel``. After signing in, select **Continue Cancellation**, complete or skip the optional survey, and confirm the cancellation. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

Remember that cancellation does not necessarily remove your Club Pogo benefits immediately. The current EA Help documentation states that you can continue playing until the end of your existing subscription period, and you should receive an email confirming the cancellation. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

If you cannot find the cancellation option, first verify that you are using the correct account and check whether the account is a paid Club Pogo account or a free account. If problems remain, use official EA Help rather than relying on unofficial websites or third-party services.

By following these steps and keeping your cancellation confirmation, you can stop future subscription renewals while retaining your Pogo account and avoiding unnecessary complications.
